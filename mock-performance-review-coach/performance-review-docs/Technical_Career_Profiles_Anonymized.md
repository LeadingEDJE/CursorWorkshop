# Technical Career Profiles

---

## Associate Solutions Developer

An Associate Solutions Developer is someone who is humble, eager to learn and highly engaged, taking advantage of the many growth opportunities the company has to offer. When working as part of a team they should be able to take on smaller tasks and take initiative to pull together the resources needed to find the right solution.

## Solutions Developer

A Solutions Developer has a proven high aptitude in at least a few technical areas and can work independently to solution small features. They know best practice and eagerly seek to grow.

## Sr. Solutions Developer

A Sr. Solutions Developer can solution large features or small apps while accounting for scalability and performance. They are able to use their technical experience to be successful in areas they are unfamiliar. They lead team collaboration, advocate for best practices and effectively communicate with non-technical team members and business stakeholders.

## Sr. Solutions Developer II

A Sr. Solutions Developer II can walk into any technical area and be successful. They are able to solution small to medium scale systems and help drive business decisions. They are able to define best practice for a team or solution. They are self aware, patient, non-reactive leaders who earn the trust of their teams and clients. They look for opportunities to elevate others.

## Principal Solutions Engineer

A Principal Solutions Engineer is an expert in providing technical vision to solve specific business problems. They are confident in providing solutions and estimates, with high level knowledge of the technologies involved. Can lead technical solutions for multiple applications and large scale (enterprise) systems. They must have the foresight to carve their own path with minimal guidance.

## Principal Solutions Engineer II

A Principal Solutions Engineer II uses their wide breadth of experience to provide the best technical vision to solve any business problem in multiple domains. They are able to provide strategic vision and guidance to multiple teams and C-Level partners.
