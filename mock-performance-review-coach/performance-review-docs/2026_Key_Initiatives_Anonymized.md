# Five Strategic Priorities for 2026: Innovation, Efficiency & Growth

The following outlines our strategic focus areas for 2026 — the priorities that will shape how we work, grow, and deliver value as a team. Each priority reflects a deliberate investment in our people, processes, and capabilities.

---

## 1. Reimagine "Down time" as a Structured Innovation Engine

Evolve our dedicated creative time from open exploration into a purposeful incubator — one that nurtures fresh ideas, encourages experimentation, and provides a clear path from concept to working prototype.

## 2. Accelerate Talent Acquisition and Onboarding for Delivery Teams

Build a more efficient, repeatable pipeline for bringing new talent on board, reducing ramp-up time and ensuring contributors can hit the ground running from day one.

## 3. Launch a Skills Mastery and Career Development Program

Define meaningful career trajectories and equip team members with the tools and learning opportunities needed to deepen their expertise and advance professionally.

## 4. Establish Recognized Expertise in Emerging Technologies

Prioritize targeted training and hands-on research so our teams become genuine authorities in the technologies shaping our industry — raising the bar for what expertise looks like in this space.

## 5. Improve Knowledge Accessibility Across the Delivery Organization

Consolidate documentation and shared resources into a more discoverable, well-organized system so every team member can quickly find the information they need to do their best work.
